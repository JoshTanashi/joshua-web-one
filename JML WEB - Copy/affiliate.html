FAQ

How does the promoter code work?

We assign you a unique code once your application is approved. Clients add that code when booking so we can track your commission.

When do I get paid?

Payments are processed monthly between the 1st–3rd for confirmed bookings that have paid a deposit.

What counts as a confirmed lead?

A lead becomes confirmed when the client pays a deposit and the booking is recorded.

Do I need prior experience?

No — anyone can join. We provide guidance and assets to help you promote effectively.

How active should I be?

Activity levels vary — we reward consistent, genuine promotion. More active promoters often earn more.

How do I track my commissions?

We will keep records and contact you monthly with payouts. For the initial stage, approved promoters receive a welcome email with tracking details.

📌 JML Photography – Promoter Rules & Expectations

  • Always use your promoter code — Every client you speak to must have your code so your commission is tracked.
  • Every lead must be submitted through the form — No form = no commission.
  • Be respectful & professional — No spamming, no rude messages, no forcing clients.
  • Honesty is essential — Don’t claim bookings you didn’t bring in. Don’t edit client quotes or make promises I didn’t approve.
  • No creating your own prices — Only use the official packages from JML Photography.
  • You may not pose as the photographer — Introduce yourself as a promoter.
  • Commission is paid on confirmed bookings only — A client must pay a deposit before commission is earned.
  • Payment is made monthly — Payments processed between the 1st–3rd of every month.
  • Be active and helpful — Answer questions quickly but professionally.
  • Respect client privacy — Never send client information to other people.
